## 1. Problem and motivation (§1)

3D character animation is expensive expert work. Neural models (Holden et al. 2016; Petrovich et al. 2022; Raab et al. 2022) and more recently motion diffusion (Kim et al. 2022; Tevet et al. 2022b) made it cheaper — **but effectively only for humanoids**, because those are the only skeletons with datasets. The paper's framing: motion data is scarce and "for a non-human skeleton, it is barely existent"; the few available datasets "contain humanoids only, whose topology and bone ratio are fixed."

So the paper attacks the **N=1** regime. Single-instance learning already exists in imaging (SinGAN, InGAN) and once in motion (Ganimator), and all of it is GAN-based. SinMDM's bet is that **diffusion is a better fit for single-input learning than GAN**, because "the descriptive ability attained by gradual denoising yields a lightweight model that, compared to prior art, is simpler in architecture and more efficient in terms of the number of parameters and training time."

**The obstacle** is stated in two places. **§1**: "vanilla transformers are not suitable for learning a single sequence, as their receptive field encompasses the entire motion" — and the same problem afflicts the UNet, whose "depth, combined with global attention layers, induces a receptive field that covers the whole motion." **§4** restates it for the UNet specifically: training one on a single input "leads to significant overfitting due to its large receptive field, resulting in synthesized sequences that closely resemble the input."

---

## 2. The core idea — narrow receptive field as the anti-overfitting mechanism (§4)

**This is the whole paper in one sentence:** *internal motifs are learned more effectively with a limited receptive field*, so the denoiser is deliberately built so that its receptive field covers only **a portion** of the input sequence, letting the network "simultaneously learn from multiple local temporal motion segments" (Fig. 2 Left).

This is the motion analogue of the **patch-based discriminator** (Isola et al. 2017; Li & Wand 2016) that SinGAN uses. Two design moves get there:

1. **Shrink UNet depth.** Receptive-field width is controlled "by tweaking the depth of the UNet". The shipped model is **d = 1** (Tab. 4 row 2); the wide-field control is **d = 3** (row 1).
2. **Replace global attention with QnA local attention.** Depth alone is insufficient, because "standard UNets employ global attention layers, resulting in a receptive field that encompasses the entire sequence."

**Why QnA and not plain windowed attention:** ViT-style non-overlapping windows "tend to limit the cross-window interaction, compromising the model's performance." **QnA** (Arar et al. 2022) is a shift-invariant local attention layer that "aggregates the input locally in an overlapping manner, much like convolutions, but with the expressive power of attention," using **learned queries shared by all windows**, which gives linear space complexity (Fig. 14; formalised in Appendix B, Eqs. 7–8). The paper's claim is precisely scoped: "**Our QnA-based UNet is the first to be used in the motion domain**."

**Explicitly rejected:** pyramids. "While traditional single-instance techniques use a pyramid of down-sampled instances … our model introduces a simple architecture that requires no pyramids." Ganimator uses both a temporal hierarchy of motions *and* a skeletal hierarchy of joints; SinMDM uses **neither**.

> ⚠️ **The paper never states the receptive field in frames.** All 18 mentions of "receptive field" are qualitative or expressed as UNet depth. From Tab. 5 we know `kernel_size = 3`, `num_res_blocks = 1`, `channel_mult = 1`. A concrete frame count is **not derivable from the paper text** — it must come from the released code if we want to replicate the mechanism.

## 3. Formal setup (§3)

### 3.1 Diffusion (§3.1) — standard DDPM, MDM-style prediction target
- **Eq. (1)** Markov forward noising, of the standard DDPM form `q(x_t | x_{t-1}) = N(√α_t · x_{t-1}, (1 − α_t) I)`, with α_t ∈ (0,1) constant hyper-parameters; when α_t is small enough, `x_T ≈ N(0, I)`.
- **Eq. (2)** The network predicts the **clean motion**, not the noise, following Tevet et al. [2022b] / Ramesh et al. [2022]: `x̂₀ = G(x_t, t)`.
- **Eq. (3)** Plain diffusion loss over an expectation in t: `L_simple = E_{x₀, t∼[1,T]}[ ‖ x₀ − G(x_t, t) ‖²₂ ]`.
- Inference: from pure noise x_T, iteratively predict x̂₀ then re-noise to x_{t−1}, until t = 0.

> ⚠️ **All six body equations are RECONSTRUCTIONS, not quotations.** This PDF's math glyphs extract as empty in every mode tried (§14). The forms above are plausible given the surrounding prose and the standard DDPM literature, but the symbol letters (including `G`) and the exact coefficient (`α_t` vs `ᾱ_t`, and whether the √ is present) are **not recoverable from the file**. **Eqs. (4) and (5) are the riskiest — see the flags below.** Open the rendered PDF before any of this enters the thesis.

### 3.2 Motion representation (§3.2) — **the part that matters for our tokenizer**
A motion splits into **dynamic features D** (per-frame, e.g. joint rotation angles) and **static features S** (temporally fixed, e.g. bone lengths). FK combines them into global 3D pose sequences. **SinMDM synthesises D only and leaves S intact** — i.e. fixed topology, fixed bone lengths. D is a tensor of T frames × F features; **convolution is 1-D over the temporal dimension**.

Two representations are used:
- **HumanML3D** (Guo et al. 2022), adopted as-is — redundant: root angular velocity about Y, root linear velocities on XZ, root height, plus local joint positions (R³), velocities (R³) and rotations (R⁶) relative to root, plus **4 binary foot-contact features** (two per leg).
- **Everything else**, adopted from Ganimator for fair comparison: "a 3D root location, a rotation angle for each joint, and foot contact labels", giving a per-frame feature count of the form **3 + (joints × rotational features) + (contact joints)**. **6D rotations are used (the paper states "6D rotation features (K = 6)", Zhou et al. 2019)**, chosen because they yield "the best result in many works".

> ⚠️ The symbol letters `J`, `K`, `B` above are **my labels**; in the PDF they are blank glyphs. The extracted text shows exactly two blank tokens after "3 +", consistent with a juxtaposed product plus one more term — i.e. `F = 3 + J·K + B` is a plausible reconstruction, **not a quotation**.

**Foot contact, Eq. (4):** where a dataset does not supply labels, they are computed as in Ganimator by **thresholding the FK-derived joint velocity** — the paper says "FK(·) denotes the **velocity** of joint j in frame t retrieved by a forward kinematics operator", and the label is an indicator that this velocity falls below a threshold. **The threshold is never given a numeric value.**

> ⚠️ Do not write Eq. (4) as a formula from this summary. The paper's operator carries a time-derivative and an argument structure that the text dump loses; only the *prose* description above is safe.

> 📌 The topology-generality claim is doing real work here: "a human, a spider, and a snake possess different values of" the contact-joint count. The representation is what makes arbitrary topology possible — nothing in the network is skeleton-specific, because the network never sees the skeleton, only D.

## 4. Applications — six, all at inference time (§5)

The headline process claim: "**All our applications are applied at inference time, with no need to re-train**", in contrast to Ganimator, "which requires specialized training for most of its applications." And (§1): tasks that need "different dedicated algorithms in prior art, are here grouped together as special cases of the same technique."

### 4.1 Motion Composition (§5.1) — one masking trick, four use cases
Given reference motion M and an ROI mask m, synthesise x̂₀ such that the masked region is generated from noise while the complement stays as close as possible to M. At each denoising step, the known part is written back into the prediction. *(The paper states this as an assignment onto the complement of the mask; the "x̂₀ ← x̂₀⊙m + M⊙(1−m)" form is an equivalent paraphrase, not the paper's notation.)*

**The one practical fix:** with a hard binary mask (Avrahami et al. 2022), "as the reference motion M deviates from the motion the model was trained on, the blending between the given and synthesized parts becomes less smooth." So they make the **mask borders linearly interpolated** rather than binary (Fig. 3).

- **Temporal composition** → *in-betweening* (mask the interior; Fig. 4) and *motion expansion* (mask the outside — the motion-domain analogue of image outpainting; Fig. 5).
- **Spatial composition** → *trajectory control* and *joints control*, by putting joint indices in the mask (Fig. 6: upper body does a warm-up from an unseen reference, lower body walks a curve from the learned motion).
- Masks can be **both spatial and temporal at once**.
- **The reference motion can be arbitrary — the model need not have been trained on it.**

### 4.2 Motion Harmonization (§5.2) — and style transfer as its special case
Composition *preserves* the injected part; harmonization *changes* it to match the learned motifs. Following **ILVR (Choi et al. 2021)**, a portion of unseen motion M is written into the synthesised sequence, and at each denoising step the **high-frequency detail of the sample is combined with the low-frequency content of the noised guidance** via a linear low-pass filter φ — **Eq. (5)**.

> ⚠️ **Eq. (5) is the least recoverable item in the paper.** The setup sentence's two noised quantities and their arguments are blank in the dump, so which term is guidance and which is sample cannot be determined from the text. The ILVR-standard form `x_{t−1} ← φ(y_{t−1}) + x_{t−1} − φ(x_{t−1})` is what the shape suggests, but **treat it as a hypothesis**. The *prose* claim — low frequencies of the guidance are injected at each denoising step — is safe and is confirmed by the Fig. 7 caption: "we follow Choi et al. [2021] and add its low frequencies at each denoising step."

**Style transfer** falls out by using *all* of M instead of a portion: the model is trained on the **style** motion, the **content** motion is unseen, and harmonization yields content-with-style (Fig. 8). The paper stresses this is non-obvious (§1): "implementing style transfer using a denoising model is a non-trivial task, and enabling it through motion harmonization is unique."

### 4.3 Free applications (§5.3)
- **Long sequences.** "Imputed to its small receptive field, the model can hallucinate a sequence as long as requested." Fig. 9: trained on a **10 s** walk-turn-walk-back clip, synthesises **60 s**. *(The narrow-RF design paying a second dividend — it is also why the model is length-agnostic.)*
- **Crowd animation.** One sequence in, a whole herd out, each sample from a different Gaussian noise (Fig. 10: jaguars, horses, ostriches).

## 5. Evaluation (§6) — what is actually measured, and with what

Hardware: experiments were "held … using an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2080 Ti GPU" (§6); Appendix A is the cleaner citation — "Our models have been trained on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2080 Ti GPU." The word *single* comes from §1's separate claim that "SinMDM can be trained on a single mid-range GPU." Data: **HumanML3D**, **Mixamo**, **Truebones Zoo**, plus one artist-created animation.

### 5.1 Why two benchmarks (§6.1)
- **Mixamo**: 70 characters, each with **unique bone lengths and some with unique topologies**; motions "more diverse and more dynamic". Used because Ganimator used it — §6.2: "For a fair comparison, we use the metrics suggested by them."
- **HumanML3D**: fixed **SMPL** topology with the mean body. Used because it is the only one of the two that supports **deep-feature** metrics, and "the usage of deep features is the current best practice [Zhang et al. 2018]."

### 5.2 The metrics (§6.2) — verbal definitions only

**Mixamo set — raw motion features, no feature-extracting model.** *"This group of metrics is applied to the motion itself, and not to deep features."*

|---|---|---|---|---|

📌 **Inter- and intra-diversity live in both sets** — same concept, different feature space (raw for Mixamo, LPIPS-over-deep-features for HumanML3D). Only **SiFID** is exclusive to the deep-feature side.

**The aggregation — Eq. (6), Harmonic Mean.** The stated rationale is the strongest methodological idea in the evaluation: *"attaining a good score on some metrics, but a bad score on others, is insufficient: **Good diversity scores with bad fidelity indicate deviation from the input motion, while good fidelity scores with bad diversity suggest overfitting.**"* So they aggregate, following Chinchor 1992 / Rijsbergen 1979 / Taha & Hanbury 2015, as a harmonic mean over **normalised** scores, "where n is the number of metrics in a table and s is the normalized score of metric i."

> ⚠️ **(A) The normalisation makes HM non-portable, and the authors do not flag it.** Scores are normalised "between zero to the metric's maximum value. **If the maximum is not known, we select the 90% percentile of the computed scores.**" For metrics where lower is better they subtract the score from the maximum — hence "a negative value is therefore valid" (Ganimator −0.22, MDM-on-crops −1.01). Because the reference maximum is drawn from *the authors' own pool of computed scores*, **an HM from this paper cannot be compared with an HM we compute on our own data.** It is a within-table ranking device, not an absolute score.
>
> ⚠️ **(B) In Tab. 1, HM appears to aggregate EFFICIENCY metrics alongside quality metrics.** The definition says "n is the number of **metrics in a table**", and Tab. 1's nine non-HM columns include #Params, #Iterations, Iteration time and Total time. On a literal reading, **Tab. 1's headline HM gap (−0.22 vs 0.85) is partly a restatement of SinMDM's 4× efficiency advantage, not purely a quality verdict** — and it explains Ganimator's negative value, since it sits at or above the 90th-percentile reference on #Params (21.7) and Total time (6.0), which normalises to ≈0 or below and drags the reciprocal sum sharply negative. **This is my inference, not a paper statement** — the paper never enumerates which columns enter HM. **Tabs. 2, 3 and 4 contain only quality metrics, so their HMs are clean.** ⇒ **Do not cite Tab. 1's HM as a quality score.** Cite Tab. 3's.

Protocol: "All the metrics are computed separately on each benchmark motion and then averaged. The metrics that measure time were computed on **benchmark motion number 9 only**."

> ⚠️ **The benchmark size is never stated** anywhere in the paper. "Motion number 9" implies **≥ 9**. The user study uses **8** motions, which may or may not be the same set. The only population figure in the paper is Mixamo's "70 characters", which describes the *dataset*, not the benchmark. This is a real reproducibility gap.

**This is the cleanest demonstration of the overfit↔divergence axis I have read for this project.**
- **MDM (global attention, full sequence) = total memorisation.** SiFID 0.01 and intra-div-diff 0.14 are *perfect* — because the output essentially *is* the input. Inter-diversity **0.03** exposes it. "The overfit of MDM is caused by the global attention it uses."
- **MDM trained on crops** *does* have a narrow receptive field, and duly gets the best inter-diversity (1.64) — but **SiFID 13.94** (7.5× SinMDM's) shows it has left the input distribution entirely.
- **SinMDM sits between**, and only the Harmonic Mean (0.82 vs 0.14 vs −1.01) exposes the difference.

> 📌 **Note carefully:** MDM-on-crops shows a narrow receptive field is **necessary but not sufficient**. Cropping the *data* to narrow the field breaks fidelity; narrowing the *architecture* does not. That distinction is the paper's real contribution and is easy to miss.

### 6.4 Tab. 4 — Ablation (HumanML3D). The receptive-field claim, tested.

| # | Variant | SiFID ↓ | Inter Div ↑ | Intra Div Dist ↓ | Harmonic Mean ↑ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | UNet w/ QnA, **wide r.f. (d=3)** | 0.69 | 0.20 | 0.34 | 0.54 |
| 2 | UNet w/ QnA, **narrow r.f. (d=1)** ← shipped | 1.87 | **0.73** | 0.40 | **0.82** |
| 3 | UNet (d=1) w/ **vanilla attention** | 1.88 | 0.72 | 0.45 | 0.79 |
| 4 | UNet (d=1) **w/o attention at all** | 2.03 | 0.72 | 0.43 | 0.79 |
| 5 | **Transformer** w/ QnA | 5.99 | 1.74 | 0.57 | 0.56 |

Three findings:
1. **Rows 1 vs 2 — the core claim holds.** Widening the field (d=3) drops inter-diversity **0.73 → 0.20** while *improving* SiFID (1.87 → 0.69) — the signature of overfitting. HM falls 0.82 → 0.54.
2. **Rows 2–4 — QnA helps, but the margin is small and honestly reported.** Vanilla attention gets HM 0.79, no attention gets HM 0.79, QnA gets 0.82. The paper does not oversell: "Both alternatives show plausible metric results, and yet, our QnA-based UNet (row 2) performs better." It also offers a good explanation for why global attention did *not* destroy things here: **"the absence of temporal positional embedding, enabling the generative model to identify motion patterns across various temporal regions."**
3. **Row 5 — transformer loses.** Even with QnA layers *and* relative temporal positional embeddings (Press et al. 2021; Shaw et al. 2018; Su et al. 2021) substituted for the global ones, the QnA-transformer gets **SiFID 5.99**, "indicating poor fidelity to the input motion."

> ⚠️ **Caption bug:** "Row 1 indicates overfit and **row 6** indicates divergence" — there is no row 6. It means **row 5**.
> ⚠️ **Label inconsistency:** Tab. 3 calls the column "Intra Div. **Diff.**", Tab. 4 calls it "Intra Div. **Dist.**". Same quantity — row 2 reproduces the Tab. 3 SinMDM row exactly (1.87 / 0.73 / 0.40 / 0.82), an internal consistency check that passes.
> 📌 These rows are **averages over the HumanML3D benchmark** (size unstated), not a single sequence: "All the metrics are computed separately on each benchmark motion and then averaged."

## 8. Assumptions and limitations

**Stated by the authors (§7):**
1. **Limited ability to synthesise out-of-distribution** — "the innate limitation of our method, common to all models (in all domains) that learn a single instance."
2. **Long inference time**, "due to the iterative nature of diffusion models." Called "the main limitation."
3. Future work: learn from "creatures with rather compatible skeletons" to relieve the single-input limit.

**Unstated, found in pass 3:**
4. **Static features are never synthesised.** Only D is generated; S (bone lengths, topology) is fixed. "Arbitrary topology" means *the method works for any given skeleton*, **not** that it generates skeletons. Retargeting/topology transfer is out of scope.
5. **Harmonic Mean is normalised against the authors' own score pool** → **not comparable across papers**; and **Tab. 1's HM appears to fold in efficiency metrics** (§5.2 ⚠️A, ⚠️B).
6. **Benchmark size is never reported** for either benchmark.
7. **No formulas** for any of the quality metrics. Reimplementation requires Ganimator's and SinGAN's papers/code.
8. **No numeric value for the foot-contact velocity threshold** in Eq. (4).
9. **No receptive-field size in frames** — only UNet depth as a proxy.
10. **User study is small**: 8 motions × 10 users per pairing, no stated recruitment, no inter-rater agreement, no confidence intervals, no significance test — yet §6.3 says "significantly preferred by the users."
11. **The QnA advantage is thin** (HM 0.82 vs 0.79 vs 0.79 in Tab. 4) and is reported on a benchmark of unknown size with no seeds or variance. On this evidence, *"local attention beats global attention"* is a weaker claim than *"a small, shallow, heavily-regularised network beats a big one."*
12. **Local Diversity's direction is arguably self-undermining — and Tab. 2 proves it.** §6.2 declares higher local diversity better, yet **acRNN, which the Tab. 2 caption says "exhibit[s] either overfit or divergence", posts the table maximum at 6.69**. So SinMDM "losing" Local Diversity to Ganimator (1.00 vs 1.17) plausibly indicates **greater fidelity, not a weakness**. The metric rewards divergence at the top end.
13. **Mixamo↔HumanML3D results are not directly comparable** — different representations, different feature spaces, and the encoder exists only for HumanML3D.
14. **The conclusion contradicts the method.** §7: "designed a motion denoising **transformer** with a narrow receptive field." §4 and §6.5 row 5 say the opposite. **Quote §4, never §7.**

## 10. Strengths and weaknesses (pass 3)

**Strengths — reusable by us:**
- **The overfit↔divergence framing with an aggregator.** *"Good diversity scores with bad fidelity indicate deviation from the input motion, while good fidelity scores with bad diversity suggest overfitting."* This is a better articulation of our own train/val-gap problem than anything currently in our eval plan, and **Tab. 3 is a ready-made worked example**.

- **Ablation margins (0.82 / 0.79 / 0.79) are within plausible run-to-run noise**, with no seeds or error bars anywhere in the paper.
- **The user study is under-powered and under-described** relative to the word "significantly", and §6.3 and Appendix C describe the instrument differently (pairwise vs several-clips-per-screen).
- **Benchmark composition undocumented.**
- **Tab. 2's win is 0.39 vs 0.38.**
- **Local Diversity's direction is questionable** (§8 item 12).
- **v2 is an arXiv preprint with no venue statement on any page.** See §11.

## 12. What this changes for our project

### 12.1 T1 ("generation quality is data-bound, not model-bound") — **contested, as the checklist predicted, but read the fine print**

SinMDM is the strongest counter-example available: **one sequence**, no additional data, and overfitting defeated by **architecture**. Tab. 4 rows 1 vs 2 is the direct evidence — same benchmark, same architecture family, only depth changes, and inter-diversity goes 0.20 → 0.73.

**But the honest version of the counter-argument is narrower than "architecture beats data":**
- The winning configuration also carries **`dropout = 0.5`**, `channel_mult = 1`, `num_res_blocks = 1` — a very small, very heavily regularised network. The prose credits the receptive field alone; the hyperparameters don't.
- **MDM-on-crops proves narrowing the field is not sufficient.** There is a *fidelity* price (SiFID 13.94), and the paper's whole evaluation apparatus exists to detect it.

→ **For `theses.md`:** SinMDM supports the *ratio* reading of T1 that we already moved to (model capacity / receptive field must be matched to data volume) and refutes only the *absolute* reading ("we simply need more video"). Another vote for the ratio framing, alongside A1-18, L3-22, A0-9 and the 71/87-prototype result. Write it as **"capacity–data ratio"**, not "data-bound", and cite SinMDM as the case where the ratio was fixed from the **capacity** side rather than the data side.

### 12.2 The eval plan needs correcting — **SiFID is not available to us**

`memory.txt` [2026-09] SCOPE lists "SinMDM coverage/diversity (+ FID_k/g w/ caveat)" in the core eval plan. After this read:

| Metric | Usable on our Thai-dance pipeline? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Coverage** | ✅ yes | Rate of input temporal windows reproduced. Needs only the input and the output. **Maps directly onto our token sequences** — coverage of input n-grams by generated n-grams. |
| **Inter Diversity** (Mixamo form) | ✅ yes, **highest value** | Distance between different generated samples. **This is the metric that catches our LSTM memorising**, and it needs no feature extractor, no ground-truth corpus. |
| **Intra Diversity Diff** | ✅ yes | Needs input intra-diversity as reference — we have that. |
| **Local Diversity** | ⚠️ yes, but read §8-12 first | Avg distance from generated windows to their nearest neighbours in the input. **Higher is not unambiguously better** — acRNN tops it at 6.69 while diverging. Report it, don't optimise it. |
| **Global Diversity** | ⚠️ needs work | Requires the tessellation operator that minimises L2 to the input — **defined in Ganimator, not here.** |
| **SiFID** | ❌ **no** | Requires the **Guo et al. [2022] HumanML3D motion encoder**. No equivalent exists for Thai classical dance, and using the HumanML3D encoder on our motions is exactly the **domain-mismatch failure L3-22 measured**. |
| **Harmonic Mean** | ⚠️ internal use only | Normalisation uses the 90th percentile of *our own* score pool → fine for ranking **our** ablations against each other, **invalid** for claiming parity with published SinMDM numbers. Keep efficiency metrics **out** of it (§5.2 ⚠️B). |

→ **Action:** amend the eval-plan line in `memory.txt` / `evaluation_methods.md` from "SinMDM coverage/diversity" to **"SinMDM *Mixamo-benchmark* metrics (coverage, local/global/inter/intra diversity) — raw-feature, no feature-extractor required; SiFID excluded, needs a HumanML3D-trained encoder we do not have"**. And note that **the formulas must come from Ganimator [Li et al. 2022], not from SinMDM**.

### 12.3 New reading obligation — **Ganimator is now a blocker, not optional**

Ganimator (Li et al. 2022) currently appears **nowhere in the checklist's TIER 0–2**. After this read it holds:
- the **actual formulas** for coverage, global diversity and local diversity — i.e. **the deliverable checklist item #2 was supposed to produce**;
- the **tessellation operator** behind global diversity;
- the **foot-contact velocity threshold** convention SinMDM inherits (Eq. 4).

→ **Recommend promoting Ganimator into TIER 0**, or at minimum attaching it to item #2 as a required companion read. Checklist experiment **E7** ("Coverage / LocalDiv / InterDiv / SiFID …") **cannot be implemented from SinMDM alone**, and its SiFID component should be struck per §12.2.

### 12.4 A cheap experiment this read hands us
**LSTM context-window ablation, mirroring Tab. 4 rows 1 vs 2.** SinMDM's controlled test is: hold data and everything else fixed, vary only the receptive field, and watch inter-diversity. Our autoregressive equivalent is to vary the LSTM's input window / effective context and measure **inter-sample diversity across generated token sequences** at fixed perplexity. If diversity collapses as context widens, we have reproduced their finding in our own setting — a far stronger answer to "is your model just memorising?" than a train/val gap. Costs one training sweep on data we already have.

### 12.5 Where SinMDM does *not* transfer
- **We are not in the N=1 regime.** Our corpus (per `memory.txt` Run 10: 30 videos / 10 dancers / 3 genres / 56,107 frames @ 24 fps — verify against the project's own data records before quoting) is orders of magnitude larger. The *architectural* prescription (shallow, narrow RF) is an argument about capacity-to-data ratio, not a recipe to copy.
- **SinMDM is continuous-space diffusion on rotations; we are discrete tokens + LSTM.** The receptive-field argument transfers as a *principle*, not as an implementation.
- **The applications (in-betweening, harmonization, style transfer) need a diffusion denoiser with an ROI mask.** None of it applies to an autoregressive LSTM over tokens. Cite these as *scope of the prior art*, not as things we could do.
- **Style transfer via harmonization is worth citing in Related Work** as evidence that style/content separation in motion is active and not solved by architecture alone — relevant to our descoped Phase-2 style story.

# ฉบับภาษาไทย (Thai version)

## สรุปสั้น

**SinMDM** (Raab, Leibovitch และคณะ, มหาวิทยาลัยเทลอาวีฟ · arXiv:2302.05905v2, 13 มิ.ย. 2023) คือ **diffusion model ตัวแรกที่เรียนจาก motion sequence เพียงเส้นเดียว** (N=1) แล้วสังเคราะห์ท่าเต้น/ท่าเคลื่อนไหวใหม่ที่หลากหลาย ความยาวเท่าไหร่ก็ได้ และยังคงรักษา "motif ภายใน" ของต้นฉบับไว้ ใช้ได้กับโครงกระดูกแบบไหนก็ได้ — คน สัตว์ หรือมังกร

**ประเด็นหลักคือ:** ปัญหาของการเทรนบนข้อมูลชิ้นเดียวคือ **overfitting** และวิธีแก้ของเขาไม่ใช่การหาข้อมูลเพิ่ม แต่คือ **ทำให้ receptive field ของ denoiser แคบลง** — ใช้ UNet ตื้น ๆ (**depth = 1**) แทน attention ทั่วทั้ง sequence ด้วย **QnA local attention** ผลคือเน็ตเวิร์ก "เรียนจากหลาย ๆ ช่วงเวลาสั้น ๆ พร้อมกัน" แทนที่จะจำทั้งเส้นได้

## ⚠️ สองข้อแก้ให้ checklist (TIER 0 ข้อ 2) — สำคัญทั้งคู่

### 1. **เปเปอร์นี้ไม่มีสูตรของ Coverage / LocalDiv / InterDiv / SiFID เลยสักตัว**

checklist ขอ *"นิยาม metric 4 ตัว (สูตรจริง ไม่ใช่ชื่อ)"* — **ไม่มีในเปเปอร์นี้**

§6.2 ให้แค่ **คำอธิบายเป็นประโยคเดียวต่อ metric** เปเปอร์มีสมการทั้งหมด **8 สมการ** คือ (1)–(3) diffusion, (4) foot contact, (5) harmonization, (6) harmonic mean, (7)–(8) QnA ใน Appendix B — **ไม่มีสมการไหนเป็นสูตร metric** (ตรวจครบทั้ง 14 หน้ารวม appendix แล้ว)

- Coverage / Global Div / Local Div → **มาจาก Ganimator [Li et al. 2022]** ("For a fair comparison, we use the metrics suggested by them")
- SiFID → **มาจาก SinGAN [Shaham et al. 2019]**
- Inter / Intra diversity → เขานิยามเอง **แต่เป็นร้อยแก้วล้วน**

**→ ถ้าจะ implement ต้องไปอ่าน Ganimator กับ SinGAN เอง เปเปอร์นี้ไม่พอ**

### 2. **"metric ที่ไม่ต้องมี corpus" จริงครึ่งเดียว — SiFID ต้องมี feature extractor**

ผู้เขียนเขียนเส้นแบ่งนี้ไว้เองใน **§1**:

> "The first is built upon the artistically crafted MIXAMO [2021] dataset, utilizing **metrics that do not require an additional feature-extracting model**. The second is based on the HumanML3D [2022] dataset and enables metrics that use latent features, such as single-FID."

- **ชุด Mixamo** (coverage, global/local/inter/intra diversity) = คิดบน **raw motion features** — "applied to the motion itself, and not to deep features" → **ใช้กับเราได้** ✅
- **ชุด HumanML3D** (**SiFID**, inter div, intra div diff) = คิดบน **deep features จาก motion encoder ของ Guo et al. [2022]** และใช้ **LPIPS** → **เราไม่มี encoder แบบนี้สำหรับรำไทย** ❌

**สองชุดนี้ซ้อนทับกัน ไม่ใช่แยกขาด** — inter/intra diversity อยู่ทั้งสองชุด ต่างกันที่ feature space **มีแค่ SiFID ที่อยู่ฝั่ง deep feature อย่างเดียว**

หมายเหตุเพิ่ม §6.2: "The aforementioned metrics are **measured relative to the input motion sequence**" → metric ชุด Mixamo อ้างอิง input ไม่ใช่ "ไม่ต้องมีอะไรเลย" แต่ไม่ต้องมี corpus ภายนอกและไม่ต้องมีโมเดลสกัดฟีเจอร์

| **Coverage** | ✅ ได้ | ใช้แค่ input กับ output **แมปตรงกับ token sequence ของเรา** (coverage ของ n-gram) |
| **Inter Diversity** (แบบ Mixamo) | ✅ ได้ **คุ้มที่สุด** | ระยะห่างระหว่าง sample ที่ generate มาคนละครั้ง **นี่คือ metric ที่จับได้ว่า LSTM เราท่องจำ** ไม่ต้องมี encoder ไม่ต้องมี ground truth |
| **Intra Diversity Diff** | ✅ ได้ | ใช้ intra-diversity ของ input เป็นตัวอ้าง ซึ่งเรามี |
| **Local Diversity** | ⚠️ ได้ แต่ดูข้อเตือนข้างบนก่อน | **สูงไม่ได้แปลว่าดีเสมอ** — acRNN ได้สูงสุดตอนที่มัน diverge รายงานได้ แต่อย่า optimise ตาม |
| **Global Diversity** | ⚠️ ต้องทำงานเพิ่ม | ต้องมี tessellation operator ที่ minimise L2 กับ input — **นิยามอยู่ใน Ganimator ไม่ใช่ที่นี่** |
| **SiFID** | ❌ **ไม่ได้** | ต้องใช้ **motion encoder ของ Guo et al. [2022] (HumanML3D)** ไม่มีตัวเทียบสำหรับรำไทย และการเอา encoder ของ HumanML3D มาใช้กับท่าเรา **คือ domain mismatch ที่ L3-22 วัดไว้แล้วเป๊ะ ๆ** |
| **Harmonic Mean** | ⚠️ ใช้ภายในเท่านั้น | normalise ด้วย percentile 90 ของ pool ตัวเอง → จัดอันดับ ablation **ของเรา** ได้ แต่ **อ้างว่าเทียบเท่าตัวเลขที่ตีพิมพ์ไม่ได้** และอย่าเอา metric ความเร็วเข้าสูตร |

**→ ต้องทำ:** แก้บรรทัดแผน eval ใน `memory.txt` / `evaluation_methods.md` จาก "SinMDM coverage/diversity" เป็น **"metric ชุด *Mixamo-benchmark* ของ SinMDM (coverage, local/global/inter/intra diversity) — raw feature ไม่ต้องมีโมเดลสกัดฟีเจอร์; ตัด SiFID ออก เพราะต้องมี encoder ที่เทรนบน HumanML3D ซึ่งเราไม่มี"** และระบุว่า **สูตรต้องไปเอาจาก Ganimator [Li et al. 2022] ไม่ใช่จาก SinMDM**

### 3. ภาระการอ่านใหม่ — **Ganimator กลายเป็นตัวบล็อก ไม่ใช่ตัวเลือก**

ตอนนี้ Ganimator (Li et al. 2022) **ไม่ปรากฏใน TIER 0–2 ของ checklist เลย** แต่มันถือ:
- **สูตรจริง** ของ coverage / global div / local div — **คือของที่ checklist ข้อ 2 ตั้งใจจะได้**
- **tessellation operator** ที่อยู่เบื้องหลัง global diversity
- **เกณฑ์ threshold ความเร็วสำหรับ foot contact** ที่ SinMDM รับมา (Eq. 4)

**→ เสนอให้เลื่อน Ganimator ขึ้น TIER 0** หรืออย่างน้อยผูกไว้กับข้อ 2 เป็นเปเปอร์คู่ที่ต้องอ่าน · **การทดลอง E7** ("Coverage / LocalDiv / InterDiv / SiFID …") **ทำจาก SinMDM อย่างเดียวไม่ได้** และส่วน SiFID ควรถูกตัดออกตามข้อ 2

### 4. การทดลองราคาถูกที่ได้มาจากการอ่านรอบนี้
**LSTM context-window ablation — ล้อ Tab. 4 แถว 1 vs 2 ตรง ๆ** วิธีทดสอบของเขาคือ ตรึงข้อมูลและทุกอย่างไว้ เปลี่ยนแค่ receptive field แล้วดู inter-diversity · ฝั่งเราคือ **เปลี่ยนขนาด context ของ LSTM แล้ววัด inter-sample diversity ของ token sequence ที่ generate ออกมา ที่ perplexity เท่ากัน** ถ้า diversity ตกเมื่อ context กว้างขึ้น = เราทำซ้ำผลของเขาได้ในบริบทของเราเอง **และเป็นคำตอบต่อคำถาม "โมเดลคุณแค่ท่องจำหรือเปล่า" ที่แข็งกว่า train/val gap มาก** · ใช้แค่ training sweep เดียวบนข้อมูลที่มีอยู่แล้ว

### 5. ส่วนที่ **ไม่** ถ่ายทอดมาถึงเรา
- **เราไม่ได้อยู่ในสภาวะ N=1** corpus เรา 30 วิดีโอ / 10 นักเต้น / 3 แนว / 56,107 เฟรม @ 24 fps (ตาม `memory.txt` Run 10 — ยืนยันกับข้อมูลจริงก่อนอ้าง) คำสั่งเชิงสถาปัตยกรรม (ตื้น, RF แคบ) เป็นข้อโต้แย้งเรื่อง**อัตราส่วน capacity ต่อข้อมูล** ไม่ใช่สูตรให้ลอกตาม
- **SinMDM คือ diffusion บน continuous rotation ส่วนเราคือ discrete token + LSTM** ข้อโต้แย้งเรื่อง RF ถ่ายทอดมาในฐานะ *หลักการ* ไม่ใช่ implementation
- **applications ทั้งหมด (in-betweening, harmonization, style transfer) ต้องมี diffusion denoiser + ROI mask** ไม่มีอันไหนใช้กับ LSTM แบบ autoregressive ได้ → อ้างในฐานะ *ขอบเขตของงานก่อนหน้า* ไม่ใช่สิ่งที่เราทำได้
- **style transfer ผ่าน harmonization ควรอ้างใน Related Work** เป็นหลักฐานว่าการแยก style/content ในโดเมน motion ยังเป็นปัญหาเปิด และแก้ด้วยสถาปัตยกรรมอย่างเดียวไม่ได้ — เกี่ยวกับเรื่อง style ของ Phase 2 ที่เรา descope ไป

## จุดอ่อนที่ต้องระบุเวลาอ้างเปเปอร์นี้

1. **ไม่มีสูตร metric เลย** — สำหรับเปเปอร์ที่ชูว่าเสนอ "benchmark ครบถ้วน 2 ชุด" นี่คือข้อบกพร่องจริง
2. **HM ไม่ใช่คะแนนสัมบูรณ์** และเปเปอร์ไม่เตือนผู้อ่าน · **HM ของ Tab. 1 น่าจะรวม metric ความเร็วเข้าไปด้วย**
3. **ช่องว่างใน ablation (0.82 / 0.79 / 0.79) อยู่ในระดับ noise ระหว่างรัน** และ**ทั้งเปเปอร์ไม่มี seed, ไม่มี error bar, ไม่มี CI, ไม่มีการทดสอบนัยสำคัญเลย** — รวมถึงคำว่า "significantly preferred" ใน user study
4. **user study เล็กและอธิบายไม่ครบ** (8 ท่า × 10 คนต่อคู่) และ §6.3 กับ Appendix C อธิบายเครื่องมือไม่ตรงกัน (เทียบเป็นคู่ vs หลายคลิปต่อหน้าจอ)
5. **ไม่บอกว่า benchmark มีกี่ท่า** (รู้แค่ว่า ≥ 9 จาก "benchmark motion number 9")
6. **ไม่บอกขนาด receptive field เป็นจำนวนเฟรม** — บอกแค่ depth ของ UNet
7. **Tab. 2 ชนะแค่ 0.39 vs 0.38**
8. **ทิศทางของ Local Diversity น่าสงสัย** (acRNN ได้สูงสุดตอน diverge)
9. **§7 ขัดกับ §4** — บทสรุปเรียกโมเดลตัวเองว่า "**transformer**" ทั้งที่ §4 เถียงทั้งหน้าว่าต้องใช้ UNet และ §6.5 แถว 5 แสดงว่า transformer แพ้ **→ อ้าง §4 อย่าอ้าง §7**
